Old Age Homes

Akshaya old age homes are widely regarded as some of the best in the country, and this is largely due to our dedicated staff who work tirelessly to ensure that our residents are given the love and care they deserve. We understand that the senior citizens who come to us are in the most vulnerable phase of their life, and we are committed to making this phase a happy one for them.

Our homes provide comfortable shelter, healthy food, and medical care to our residents. In addition to this, we also organize entertainment activities, tours, and visits to temples and places of interest to keep our residents engaged and entertained. Our aim is to create an environment where our residents feel loved, cared for, and happy.

We owe our success to the excellent support of our donors who have been a constant source of encouragement and motivation. It is their generosity that has enabled us to provide the best possible care to our residents. We are grateful for their support and hope that they continue to partner with us in our mission to provide a new life to abandoned senior citizens.

Education Support Youth

Akshaya Vidyalaya is a free English medium school that follows the Montessori method of teaching and learning. We believe in nurturing the holistic development of each child, focusing not only on academic excellence but also on their emotional, social, and physical well-being.

Akshaya Trust Goshala

The Akshaya Trust Goshala is a compassionate initiative dedicated to the care and protection of cows.

“ This project reflects the Trust’s commitment to animal welfare and traditional values of compassion. By supporting the Goshala, donors help sustain feeding programs, veterinary care, and shelter facilities that ensure dignity and comfort for these animals. The Goshala also serves as a space for community engagement, encouraging people to participate in seva (service) and learn about sustainable practices in cattle care.”
