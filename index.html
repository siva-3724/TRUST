FAQ

01

What is akshaya trust chennai& Kancheepuram?

Akshaya Trust is a charitable organization serving Chennai and Kancheepuram. We provide free old age homes, education through Akshaya Vidyalaya, and community welfare programs.

02

what facilities are provided in the old age homes?

Residents receive clean shelter, nutritious food, medical care, personal lockers, uninterrupted power, entertainment, festival celebrations, and regular tours all free of cost.

03

Is akshaya trust transparent and recognized?

Yes. The Trust is registered under Sec 12A and Sec 80(G) of the IT Act, with FCRA registration for foreign donations. Annual reports and audits ensure accountability..
